# Code of Conduct

ö-esu-esu is a learning community. We want everyone to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or skill level.

## We expect you to

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

## Not okay

- Insults, harassment or mocking someone for a "basic" question.
- Sharing someone's privite information.
- Spam issues or pull requests.

## Reporting

If something makes you uncomfortable, message one of the ö-esu-esu organisers privately. We'll keep it confidential.
